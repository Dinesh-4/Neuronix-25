NEURONIX '25

The Pulse of Intelligence

Dive into the future of artificial intelligence at the most advanced symposium of 2025

ABOUT

The Heartbeat of Innovation

Neuronix – The Pulse of Intelligence is a movement by the AI Club, exploring the future of AI and celebrating creativity powered by machines.

Fueling Young Minds

Neuronix inspires innovation through tech events, workshops, and keynotes — a launchpad for students to shine in AI and Data Science.

A Platform for Possibilities

More than events, Neuronix is a hub for networking, collaboration, and innovation — where ideas thrive and boundaries fade.

EVENTS

Day 1

Technical Events

  • Paper Presentation
    Have an innovative idea or research in AI, ML, or Data Science? This is your platform to showcase it! Present your work as a detailed paper or a creative poster — whether it's a novel algorithm or an AI application, express your vision with clarity and impact. Choose your format, impress the experts, and let your ideas speak!
  • Poster Presentation
    Explore the future of tech with our beginner-friendly Quantum Computing Workshop at Neuronix! Learn the basics of qubits, superposition, and quantum algorithms, and discover how they’re revolutionizing AI, cryptography, and more. No prior experience needed — just curiosity and a passion to explore the next frontier of computing.
  • Prompt War
    Test your prompt engineering skills in this fast-paced AI challenge! Craft smart, creative prompts to solve logic puzzles, generate code, or create stories — and get the best responses from the AI. It’s a real-time battle of brains, judged on originality and impact. One prompt. One shot. Are you ready?
  • Tech Riddle
    Gear up for TechRiddle, the ultimate tech quiz that tests your knowledge in DSA, AI, ML, and programming! It’s a fast-paced battle of logic, speed, and smart thinking. Whether you're a coder or a curious mind, this is your chance to shine with sharp answers and quick wit.

About Day 1

Experience the cutting-edge of technology with AI-focused workshops, research presentations, and thrilling tech challenges.

Day 2

Cultural Events

  • Group Dance
    Hit the stage with your crew and let your moves speak louder than words! From classical fusion to hip-hop, Group Dance at Neuronix is all about energy, expression, and storytelling through rhythm. Own the spotlight, feel the vibe, and dance like the floor is yours!
  • Talent Show
    Got a hidden talent? This is your moment! Whether it’s singing, dancing, mimicry, or something unique, the Talent Show at Neuronix lets you shine your way. No limits, just pure self-expression — because your talent is your superpower.
  • Mic for a Minute (JAM)
    One mic. One minute. No mistakes. In this fun, fast-paced JAM challenge, speak non- stop on quirky topics without hesitation, repetition, or deviation. Think fast, talk faster — and own the mic like a pro!
  • Lyrics Hub
    Love music and never miss a lyric? Lyrics Hub is your jam! Guess songs from scrambled lines, translations, or iconic words. Test your memory, music sense, and speed — and sing your way to victory!
  • Connection
    Ready to crack clues and spot hidden links? Connection is a mind-twisting event where logic, speed, and creativity collide. Decode patterns from images, words, or symbols — and reveal the theme before anyone else!
  • Haunt and Hunt
    Enter if you dare! Haunt & Hunt is a ghost-themed treasure hunt filled with eerie clues, haunted puzzles, and spine-chilling twists. Team up, think fast, and race through the shadows to uncover the hidden treasure. Escape the dark — or get lost in it!

About Day 2

Showcase your creativity and talent through diverse cultural activities, performances, and interactive entertainment events.
