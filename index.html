ABOUT

B.TECH Artificial Intelligence and Data Science Department

The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI&DS) at Vel Tech HighTech Dr. Rangarajan Dr. Sakunthala Engineering College is committed to shaping the future of technology by nurturing the next generation of AI professionals. The department offers a well-structured curriculum that seamlessly integrates foundational computer science with emerging areas like machine learning, deep learning, data analytics, and big data technologies. Students are equipped with both theoretical knowledge and practical expertise, ensuring they are prepared to meet the demands of an ever-evolving tech industry.



The Heartbeat of Innovation

Neuronix – The Pulse of Intelligence is a movement by the AI Club, exploring the future of AI and celebrating creativity powered by machines.

Fueling Young Minds

Neuronix inspires innovation through tech events, workshops, and keynotes — a launchpad for students to shine in AI and Data Science.

A Platform for Possibilities

More than events, Neuronix is a hub for networking, collaboration, and innovation — where ideas thrive and boundaries fade.

EVENTS

Day 1

Technical Events

  • Paper Presentation
    Description:
    The Paper Presentation event offers a platform for individuals or teams to showcase their innovative ideas, research insights, or project outcomes through a technical paper. Topics can cover any domain within Artificial Intelligence and its applications, including Machine Learning, Data Science, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, AI in industry, society, and emerging technologies. The goal is to encourage original thinking, real-world relevance, and technical communication.

    Rules and Guidelines:
    Participation can be individual or in teams (maximum 3 members).
    Abstract submission is mandatory (PDF format, maximum 300 words).

    Final paper (if applicable) should follow IEEE or standard technical paper format (maximum 6 pages).

    Participants must use slides (PPT or PDF format) for presentation and bring them on a USB drive.

    Presentation Time:
    7 minutes + 3 minutes for Q&A.
    All content must be original. Plagiarism will result in disqualification.

    All team members must be present during the session.

    Judges’ decisions will be final.
    Certificates will be awarded to all presenting participants.
  • Poster Presentation
    Description:
    The Poster Presentation event invites participants to visually present their innovative ideas, projects, or research through engaging and informative posters. Topics can include any field of Artificial Intelligence and its applications such as Machine Learning, Data Science, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and related emerging domains. This format allows for concise yet impactful communication of concepts.

    Rules and Guidelines:
    Participation can be individual or in teams (maximum 3 members).
    Abstract submission is not required for poster presentations.

    Posters must be printed in A2 or A1 size and brought to the venue.

    Explanation Time:
    Maximum 5 minutes per team during evaluation.

    All content must be original. Plagiarism will result in disqualification.

    All team members must be present during their session.

    Judges’ decisions will be final.
    Certificates will be awarded to all presenting participants.
  • Prompt War
    Description:
    In this AI-powered challenge, participants will showcase their skill in prompt engineering—the craft of designing effective and intelligent instructions for AI models. From solving logical puzzles to generating creative content, participants must think quickly and communicate clearly to get the best possible outputs from the AI. It’s not just what you say—it’s how you prompt it.

    Rules and Guidelines:
    This is an individual event.
    Prompts will be crafted live based on specific tasks or categories, such as creative writing, code generation, summarization, image generation (if applicable), etc.
    AI outputs will be generated and evaluated in real-time using pre-set models/tools provided by the organizers.

    Each participant will be given 3 different tasks, with a fixed time limit (e.g., 5 minutes per prompt).

    Participants must submit their final prompt and the corresponding output within the allotted time.

    Evaluation will be based on the effectiveness, creativity, and relevance of the prompt-output pair.

    Judges’ decisions will be final. Certificates will be issued to winners and participants.
  • Tech Riddle
    Description: Techriddle is a fast-paced and brain-teasing event where participants must decode riddles, puzzles, and clues related to technology, computer science, AI, and the digital world. Whether it’s cracking logic-based challenges or identifying tech tools from cryptic hints, this event will test your wit, problem-solving ability, and tech-savviness under pressure.

    Rules and Guidelines:
    This is an individual event. Participants will be presented with a series of tech-based riddles or puzzles—ranging from logical reasoning to decoding abbreviations, identifying tech personalities, or solving coding-themed brainteasers.

    Each participant will get:
    5 riddles/tasks in total Fixed time limit (e.g., 15 minutes) to solve and submit answers.

    Riddles may include formats such as: Cryptic tech phrases. Visual/code-based hints. Logical deduction puzzles

    “Guess the gadget/person/language/tool” style questions, Use of mobile phones, internet, or external help is strictly prohibited.

    Judges’ decisions are final. Certificates will be issued to winners and participants.

Day 2

Cultural Events

  • Group Dance
    Description:
    Get ready to set the stage on fire! Group Dance is a high-energy event where rhythm meets creativity. Teams of passionate dancers will showcase their coordination, expressions, and choreography as they compete to impress the audience and judges. Whether it’s classical, hip-hop, fusion, or freestyle—bring your best moves and steal the spotlight!

    Rules and Guidelines:
    This is a team event.
    Each team can have about 7 members.

    Performance Time: Maximum: 6 minutes

    Props are allowed, but teams must bring their own and ensure safety.

    Music must be submitted in MP3 format at least 2 days before the event to the cordinator.

    Judges’ decisions are final.
    Certificates will be provided to winners and participants.
  • Talent Show
    Description:
    Everyone has a spark—Show Off is your stage to let it shine! Whether it’s dancing, singing, mimicry, beatboxing, stand-up comedy, or any other special skill, this event is all about celebrating individuality and talent. Step up solo, bring your best, and own the spotlight in front of an enthusiastic crowd!

    Rules and Guidelines:
    This is an individual event.
    Participants can perform any one talent of their choice, such as:

    Dance. Singing. Mimicry. Beatboxing. Stand-up comedy. Instrumental music. Poetry or storytelling. Magic tricks or other unique personal skills

    Performance Time:
    Maximum: 6 minutes

    Music tracks or supporting materials (if needed) must be submitted 2 days before the event in MP3 or standard format to the cordinator. Props are allowed but must be brought by the participant.

    Judges’ decisions are final. Certificates will be given to winners and participants.
  • Mic for a Minute (JAM)
    Description: Ready. Set. Speak! Mic for a Minute is a thrilling test of spontaneity, fluency, and presence of mind. Participants must speak non-stop for one minute on a surprise topic—without repetition, hesitation, or deviation. It’s a fun, fast-paced event that challenges your wit, vocabulary, and ability to think on your feet!

    Rules and Guidelines: This is an individual event. Topics will be given on the spot. Each participant must speak continuously for 1 minute on the assigned topic.

    The goal is to avoid: Repetition (no repeating words or ideas). Hesitation (no unnatural pauses, fillers like "um", "uh"). Deviation (stay on the topic without straying). A moderator will monitor errors. Participants will be stopped if they break a rule.

    Top scorers may face a final JAM round for the title.
    Judges’ decisions are final.
    Certificates will be issued to winners and participants.
  • Lyrics Hub
    Description: Think you know your favorite songs inside out? LyricsHub is the ultimate lyrical challenge where participants must identify songs from incomplete lines, twisted phrases, or hummed tunes. Whether you're a pop lover, a classical fan, or a Bollywood buff, your lyrical memory and music sense will be put to the test!

    Rules and Guidelines: This is an individual or duo event.
    Participants will be given audio clips, text snippets, or jumbled lyrics from popular songs.

    Each participant/team will face a set of 5 challenges:
    Identify the song name and/or artist.Complete the missing lyrics.Unjumble the lyrics. Guess the song from a humming/audio clip. Rapid-fire round (optional/finalists only)

    Time limit: Around 30 seconds per question.

    No use of mobile phones or external help. Any such attempt will lead to disqualification.
    Bonus points for accuracy in lyrics Judges’ decisions are final.Certificates will be awarded to winners and participants.
  • Connection
    Description:
    Connection is a mind-bending quiz event where logic meets creativity. Participants are presented with a series of clues—images, words, symbols, or phrases—and must find the hidden link that connects them all. It’s not just about what you see—it’s about how you think. Decode, connect, and conquer!

    Rules and Guidelines: This is a team event. Each team can have 2–3 members.
    The event consists of multiple rounds such as:
    Visual Round: Identify the common connection between 3–4 images.
    Verbal Round: Connect a set of words, names, or facts.
    Audio/Video Round: Guess the theme from sound clips or short video clues.

    Final Rapid Connect Round (for top-scoring teams): Quick-fire connections with increasing difficulty.

    Each question will have a time limit (e.g., 30–60 secs).
    No use of mobile phones or internet during the event. Judges’/Quizmaster's decisions are final. Certificates will be given to winners and participants.
  • Haunt & Hunt – Treasure Hunt
    Description:
    Get ready to dive into a world of mystery, clues, and adrenaline! Haunt & Hunt is not your typical treasure hunt—it’s a race against time packed with riddles, haunted twists, and mind-boggling tasks. Participants must solve clues, decode messages, and uncover hidden secrets across the venue to reach the final treasure. Only the smartest, fastest, and most daring team will win the ultimate prize!

    Rules and Guidelines:
    This is an individual event. Participants will be given the first clue to begin the hunt. Solving each clue leads to the next location or challenge.

    The event will include:
    Riddle-solving. Physical challenges or tasks. Observation and logic-based puzzles.

    Themed zones (e.g., “haunted” checkpoints) for added thrill. Total number of checkpoints: 5 to 7, depending on the venue and time.

    Time limit: participants must complete the hunt within the given time (e.g., 45–60 minutes).

    Rules to follow:
    No use of mobile phones or external help. Do not damage any property or clue materials. Stick together as a team—splitting is not allowed. Cheating or tampering with other team clues leads to disqualification. The team that reaches the final treasure first (with all clues completed correctly) will be declared the winner.
    Judges' decisions are final. Certificates will be awarded to winners and all participants.
